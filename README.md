# Lead Generation Systems

## The Goal

Generate predictable, qualified pipeline.

Not random leads.

Not vanity metrics.

Revenue-producing opportunities.

---

## Core Components

### Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Define:

- Industry
- Company Size
- Revenue Range
- Growth Stage
- Buying Signals

---

### Lead Sources

- Outbound
- Referrals
- Partnerships
- LinkedIn
- Communities
- Content

---

### Qualification Framework

Questions:

- Do they have a problem?
- Do they have urgency?
- Do they have budget?
- Do they have authority?
- Do they have a compelling event?

---

### Lead Scoring

Evaluate:

- Fit
- Intent
- Engagement
- Timing

---

### Outreach Framework

Pain → Insight → Value → Conversation

---

## Metrics

- Leads Generated
- Meetings Booked
- SQL Conversion
- Pipeline Created
- Revenue Generated

---

## Common Mistakes

- Weak ICP
- Generic messaging
- No qualification process
- Activity without strategy

---

## Principle

More leads do not guarantee more revenue.

Better leads do.
