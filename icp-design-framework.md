# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Design Framework

## Purpose

The quality of lead generation depends on the quality of targeting.

A strong ICP helps revenue teams focus on prospects most likely to buy, retain, and expand.

---

## What Is an ICP?

An Ideal Customer Profile represents the type of company that receives the highest value from your solution.

A strong ICP improves:

- Conversion rates
- Win rates
- Retention
- Revenue growth

---

## Firmographic Criteria

Evaluate:

### Industry

Examples:

- SaaS
- FinTech
- E-commerce
- Consulting

---

### Company Size

Examples:

- 11-50 employees
- 51-200 employees
- 201-1000 employees

---

### Revenue

Examples:

- $1M-$10M
- $10M-$50M
- $50M-$100M

---

## Buying Signals

Look for:

- Hiring growth
- Funding announcements
- New leadership hires
- Technology investments
- Market expansion

---

## Pain Signals

Examples:

- Pipeline leakage
- CRM inefficiency
- Forecasting challenges
- Low conversion rates
- Slow sales cycles

---

## Disqualification Criteria

Avoid:

- Poor product fit
- No budget
- No urgency
- No strategic alignment

---

## ICP Scoring Model

### Fit Score

0-50 Points

### Intent Score

0-30 Points

### Timing Score

0-20 Points

Maximum Score:

100

---

## Benefits

- Better targeting
- Higher conversion
- Lower acquisition cost
- Improved revenue predictability

---

## Key Principle

Lead generation improves when targeting improves.

The best campaigns begin with the right ICP.
