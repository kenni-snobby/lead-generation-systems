# Lead Scoring Model

## Purpose

Lead scoring helps sales teams prioritize opportunities most likely to convert.

The objective is simple:

Focus attention where it creates the highest revenue impact.

---

## Why Lead Scoring Matters

Without lead scoring:

- Sales teams chase every lead equally
- Response times increase
- Conversion rates decrease

Lead scoring improves prioritization.

---

## Scoring Framework

### Fit Score (50 Points)

Measures ICP alignment.

Evaluate:

- Industry
- Company Size
- Revenue Range
- Technology Stack

---

### Intent Score (30 Points)

Measures buying interest.

Evaluate:

- Demo Requests
- Pricing Page Visits
- Website Engagement
- Content Downloads

---

### Timing Score (20 Points)

Measures urgency.

Evaluate:

- Active Projects
- Hiring Activity
- Funding Events
- Technology Changes

---

## Score Ranges

### A Leads

Score: 80–100

Action:

Immediate sales outreach.

---

### B Leads

Score: 60–79

Action:

Prioritized follow-up.

---

### C Leads

Score: Below 60

Action:

Automated nurturing.

---

## Lead Routing

### A Leads

Route to senior sales representatives.

### B Leads

Route to SDR team.

### C Leads

Route to marketing nurture programs.

---

## KPIs

Track:

- Lead-to-SQL Conversion
- SQL-to-Opportunity Conversion
- Opportunity Creation Rate
- Revenue per Lead

---

## Common Mistakes

- Overcomplicated scoring models
- No ICP alignment
- Ignoring buying signals
- No periodic review

---

## Review Cadence

Monthly:

- Review conversion performance
- Adjust scoring rules
- Validate ICP assumptions

---

## Key Principle

Not every lead deserves the same level of attention.

Prioritization drives productivity.
