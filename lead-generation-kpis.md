# Lead Generation KPIs

## Purpose

Lead generation should be measured by revenue impact.

Not activity volume.

The objective is predictable pipeline creation.

---

## Core Lead Generation Metrics

### Leads Generated

Definition:

Total leads acquired during a specific period.

Measure:

- Weekly
- Monthly
- Quarterly

---

### Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs)

Definition:

Leads meeting predefined engagement criteria.

Examples:

- Demo requests
- High website activity
- Content engagement

---

### Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs)

Definition:

Leads validated by the sales team.

Criteria:

- Problem identified
- Fit confirmed
- Sales readiness established

---

## Conversion Metrics

### Lead → MQL Conversion

Formula:

MQLs ÷ Leads Generated × 100

---

### MQL → SQL Conversion

Formula:

SQLs ÷ MQLs × 100

---

### SQL → Opportunity Conversion

Formula:

Opportunities ÷ SQLs × 100

---

### Opportunity → Customer Conversion

Formula:

Customers ÷ Opportunities × 100

---

## Pipeline Metrics

### Pipeline Created

Definition:

Revenue value of opportunities generated.

---

### Pipeline Coverage

Formula:

Pipeline Value ÷ Revenue Target

Benchmark:

3x–5x

---

### Revenue Contribution

Definition:

Revenue attributed to lead generation efforts.

---

## Efficiency Metrics

### Cost Per Lead (CPL)

Formula:

Marketing Spend ÷ Leads Generated

---

### Cost Per Opportunity

Formula:

Marketing Spend ÷ Opportunities Created

---

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Formula:

Sales + Marketing Spend ÷ Customers Acquired

---

## Quality Metrics

Track:

- Win Rate
- Sales Cycle Length
- Deal Size
- Retention Rate

---

## KPI Review Cadence

### Weekly

- Lead Volume
- Conversion Trends

### Monthly

- Pipeline Created
- Opportunity Creation

### Quarterly

- Revenue Impact
- CAC
- ROI

---

## Common Mistakes

- Measuring volume instead of quality
- Ignoring conversion rates
- Tracking activity without outcomes
- Focusing on vanity metrics

---

## Key Principle

The goal of lead generation is not more leads.

The goal is more qualified revenue opportunities.
