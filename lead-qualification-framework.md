# Lead Qualification Framework

## Purpose

Lead qualification helps revenue teams focus on opportunities most likely to convert.

The objective is not more opportunities.

The objective is better opportunities.

---

## Why Qualification Matters

Poor qualification creates:

- Forecast inaccuracy
- Pipeline inflation
- Wasted sales effort
- Longer sales cycles

Strong qualification improves revenue predictability.

---

## Qualification Framework

### Problem

Questions:

- What challenge are they facing?
- How is it impacting the business?
- What happens if nothing changes?

---

### Urgency

Questions:

- Why now?
- What is driving the initiative?
- Is there a deadline?

---

### Authority

Questions:

- Who is involved in the decision?
- Who owns the budget?
- Who signs the agreement?

---

### Budget

Questions:

- Has funding been allocated?
- Is budget approved?
- What investment range is realistic?

---

### Fit

Evaluate:

- Industry alignment
- Company size
- Revenue profile
- Strategic relevance

---

## Qualification Scorecard

### Problem

0–20 Points

### Urgency

0–20 Points

### Authority

0–20 Points

### Budget

0–20 Points

### Fit

0–20 Points

Maximum Score:

100

---

## Qualification Levels

### Highly Qualified

80–100

Action:

Prioritize immediately.

---

### Moderately Qualified

60–79

Action:

Continue discovery.

---

### Poorly Qualified

Below 60

Action:

Nurture or disqualify.

---

## Warning Signs

- No clear business problem
- No urgency
- No budget ownership
- No decision-making authority
- Weak strategic fit

---

## Benefits

- Better forecasting
- Faster sales cycles
- Higher win rates
- Improved pipeline quality

---

## Key Principle

A smaller pipeline of qualified opportunities is more valuable than a larger pipeline of unqualified opportunities.
