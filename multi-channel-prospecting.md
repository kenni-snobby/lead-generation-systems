# Multi-Channel Prospecting Framework

## Purpose

Prospects rarely respond through a single channel.

Modern prospecting requires coordinated outreach across multiple touchpoints.

The goal is visibility, relevance, and engagement.

---

## Why Multi-Channel Works

Prospects are busy.

Some respond to:

- Email
- LinkedIn
- Phone Calls
- Referrals
- Communities

A multi-channel approach increases response rates and meeting creation.

---

## Core Channels

### Email

Best for:

- Personalized outreach
- Problem-focused messaging
- Follow-up sequences

Goal:

Start conversations.

---

### LinkedIn

Best for:

- Visibility
- Relationship building
- Thought leadership

Goal:

Build familiarity before outreach.

---

### Phone

Best for:

- Urgent opportunities
- Qualification
- Relationship acceleration

Goal:

Create direct conversations.

---

### Referrals

Best for:

- Warm introductions
- Faster trust building

Goal:

Reduce friction.

## Messaging Framework

Structure:

Pain → Impact → Insight → Conversation

Avoid:

- Generic pitches
- Feature dumping
- Long introductions

---

## Success Metrics

Track:

- Reply Rate
- Connection Acceptance Rate
- Meeting Conversion Rate
- Pipeline Created
- Revenue Generated

---

## Common Mistakes

- Over-personalization
- Channel dependency
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Weak ICP definition

---

## Key Principle

Prospecting is not about sending more messages.

It's about creating more relevant conversations.
