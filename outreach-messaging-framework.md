# Outreach Messaging Framework

## Purpose

The goal of outreach is not to pitch.

The goal is to start a relevant conversation.

Most outbound messages fail because they focus on the seller instead of the buyer.

---

## Messaging Structure

Pain → Impact → Insight → Conversation

---

## Step 1: Pain

Identify a problem the prospect is likely experiencing.

Examples:

- Pipeline leakage
- Low conversion rates
- CRM inefficiencies
- Forecasting challenges
- Slow sales cycles

---

## Step 2: Impact

Highlight the business consequences.

Examples:

- Lost revenue
- Missed forecasts
- Higher acquisition costs
- Reduced productivity

---

## Step 3: Insight

Introduce a perspective.

Examples:

- Industry trend
- Operational observation
- Revenue opportunity
- Process improvement

---

## Step 4: Conversation

Create curiosity.

Avoid:

- Hard selling
- Long pitches
- Aggressive CTAs

Goal:

Start a discussion.

---

## Example Framework

### Weak Message

Hi John,

We help companies improve sales performance through our platform.

Would you be open to a demo?

---

### Strong Message

Hi John,

Many SaaS teams struggle with pipeline visibility as they scale.

The challenge isn't usually lead volume.

It's identifying where opportunities leak before forecasts are affected.

Curious — how is your team currently tracking pipeline health?

---

## Personalization Levels

### Level 1

Company personalization

### Level 2

Role personalization

### Level 3

Trigger-based personalization

Examples:

- Funding
- Hiring
- Expansion
- Leadership changes

---

## Success Metrics

Track:

- Open Rate
- Reply Rate
- Positive Reply Rate
- Meeting Conversion Rate
- Pipeline Created

---

## Common Mistakes

- Talking about yourself too early
- Feature-heavy messaging
- Generic templates
- Weak targeting

---

## Key Principle

Relevance earns attention.

Attention creates conversations.

Conversations create opportunities.
